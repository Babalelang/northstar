Player profiles

A sharper look at the league’s best players, their output and how the VUVA rating model sees them.

AM
Relebohile Mofokeng

The most complete creative force in the league, combining explosive dribbling, inventive passing and genuine end-product.

CM
Teboho Mokoena

A midfielder whose control of tempo and positional discipline make him central to almost every attacking phase.

CF
Iqraam Rayners

Prolific in front of goal and effective in high-press situations, with a strong instinct for the decisive action.

RW
Oswin Appollis

A pacey wide attacker whose directness and speed can tear open defensive structure in transition.
